Layout concept

Full-bleed camera shell — controls as HUD overlays

Today’s Violet Pulse mock (and similar decks) treated video as an inset stage card sitting inside a purple shell, with a solid footer below the card. This concept removes that sandwich: the whole phone face is the camera image. App name, settings, status chips, PiP, and action rows float as frosted glass over the live picture — like a walkie “SEE” viewfinder, not a chat app with a video thumbnail.

Before (stage card)

Top bar + themed chrome → rounded video card → solid footer panel. Camera is a window inside the UI.

After (full-bleed HUD)

Camera edge-to-edge. Title/settings on the image. Footer is buttons only (no tray). Flip only on the self-picture frame.

  • Do not invent new features or rename controls — only re-layout chrome as overlays.
  • Top: gradient wordmark Barky + peer subtitle + settings / contacts icons over camera.
  • Mid: status chip (e.g. “SEE live”); self PiP when dual — flip lives only inside the self-picture frame.
  • Bottom: no frosted tray — only individual buttons + HOLD floating on the camera.
  • Aesthetic: deep violet, magenta–purple HOLD, glass chips — same energy as Concept E (Violet Pulse).
